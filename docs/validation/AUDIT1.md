## 4. The block-walk overshoot under wires 26 and 27

**Claim as written.** The arithmetic certificate ([M3H6](../M3H6_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md) section 2.3 and clause (i)) bounds an intercept operand using the premise that every point of a visited cell is within 2,048 + 128 + 1 units per axis of the trace start; the slide-window certificate says a slide visits at most three cells in a 2 x 2 window; [WIRE27](WIRE27.md#6-the-blockmap-census-and-the-bound-on-the-intercept-operands) step 8 says its bound holds when the walk overshoots; WIRE27 section 7 says the overshoot was shown in a stub harness only. **Method (run).** `tools/reference/overshoot_wad.py` and `overshoot_goldens.py` (synthetic slots, the M6a runner; strict, overflow, address with leak detection disabled, witness, an `observer` build that prints every walk and changes no step, and a planted-fault build whose walk stops before any cell past its end cell). Rust is compared by DSIM43 digest after every tic (`crates/doom-sim/tests/overshoot_walk.rs`). The mechanism: a walk crossing a column and a row line steps north only when the y intercept's cell changes; if `ystep` truncates to 0 the walk steps east on every iteration, for all 64.

**Does the argument hold as written?**

- **The M3H6 premise and clause (i)'s derivation: refuted by a run.** WRAP's map passes clause (i) (and (iii) to (viii), by a scratch `ArithmeticCertificate::evaluate`) yet an admitted slide trace's intercept leaves `int` (6 genuine calls at tic 52). The bound "independent of the map's extent" does not bound an overshooting walk.
- **The conclusion for wires 17 to 22 survives, on a different clause.** WRAP is refused at load by wires 17 and 22 (scratch) only through clause (ii), the sight clause: for a line intercept the numerator is at most `256 * dist(t, v1) * L2 + L1 + 2`, so it fits whenever the trace start t lies in the vertex rectangle (read, not run). The restated premise is "the trace start lies in the vertex rectangle", not "the visited cell is within reach". **Open:** a start up to 16 units (a slide trace's corner) plus 1 (the nudge) outside the rectangle is not covered as written; no case was constructed; the reading is that a row-bound overshoot cannot also reach a line at nearly the full diagonal; **not proven either way.**
- **Wires 23 to 28** check the arithmetic at run time: an overshoot that wraps is refused, not silent (WRAP). The wires 23 to 26 `INT_MIN` divergence is the existing known gap.
- **The slide-window certificate's premise also fails** (SLIDE's slide walk visits 64 cells, not at most 3); "the slide witness never fires" is not proven; a slide collecting a 129th intercept would be refused by the witness, not silent (not constructed, read).
- **WIRE27 step 8 holds as written:** in both runs the farthest visited cell is 63 cells from the start cell and the far lines met are 3,557 and about 4,850 units away, inside its 8,320.
- **Planted faults:** the genuine reach-bounded build differs from strict first at capture 39; Rust under wires 26 to 28 differs from those digests at capture 39 (committed test); a scratch Rust walk that breaks before any cell past the end cell fails both committed tests at capture 39 (exit 101); altered digests at captures 0, 39 and 45 are reported at their capture.

**Tried:** 2 full-engine items, 4 direct calls, 810 walks cell by cell. **Not tried:** a full-engine hitscan or use overshoot, a thing in an overshoot cell, a 129th intercept, real maps, skills 0, 1 and 3, wires 1 to 25 on these items (only the load check of wires 17 and 22). A scratch search (a replica of the walk, search only, not evidence) found no overshoot of a use trace from thrust alone in 4,194,304 starts; the construction gets `ystep` 0 from a slide that zeroes y momentum followed by one tic of a small forward move. **Reachable on wire 28:** the overshoot in principle (ordinary inputs, running along a wall that lies on a BLOCKMAP line, a few raw units from it, facing within the fine-angle-0 bucket, then a small forward move), **not observed on a real map**; its consequence on wire 28 is never silent. **Recommendation:** no wire 29 for the overshoot (no silent divergence found, and the Rust walks are the C's); restate the M3H6 premise (documents only, dated notes added) and mark the slide-window "never fires" as not proven; the new quirk row is Q-p_maputl-868.

## 7. The flaky doom-play wall-clock test

**The test** is `session_writer::tests::a_stalled_disk_keeps_the_queue_bounded_and_loses_no_tic` (`crates/doom-play/src/session_writer_tests.rs`), **the one existing test this PR changes (the owner's exception of 2026-11-26)**. **The failure on record:** workflow run 45452294076, attempt 1 (PR #82, head `cac2ae37`, 2026-11-24), job "Stable / macos-latest": `an update took 747.09045ms with the disk stalled`; attempt 2 passed. **Cause (run):** the test takes the wall-clock time of each of `QUEUE_TICS * 3` calls to `app.update` and bounds the longest by 500 ms; wall-clock time includes time the thread waits for a processor. Alone the test takes 0.41 s. Built from master and run at `nice 19` on one CPU beside three busy loops it failed twice out of two (1.02 s and 1.19 s) with nothing waiting on the disk; at equal priority on one CPU it passed 6 of 6 (Linux did not reproduce it there). **What changed (measurement):** the longest update is now the least of up to three runs of the whole scenario (`least_of_three`), stopping at the first run under the bound. **What did not change:** the 500 ms bound and every assertion (each is made in every run). A new test, `the_stalled_disk_measurement_keeps_a_delay_that_repeats`, shows the measurement still fails on a delay present in every run and passes one present only in the first. **After (run):** 5 of 5 passes unstarved; under the same sustained starvation it still fails (`an update took 956.81944ms`): the change removes a one-off stall, not a starved machine. Not run: macOS locally; the CI macOS job is the only macOS observation. **Recommendation:** accept; if macOS fails again, the remaining lever is the bound itself, which is the owner's.
