## Review notes and follow-ups

- Bonus effects, cap behavior, COUNTITEM ordering and P_SpawnMapThing ordering
  (timer, kill total, item total, angle) were re-read against `p_inter.c` and
  `p_mobj.c`. Legacy spawn batches pass `count_items=false`, preserving
  historical `total_items = 0` contracts.
- The counted-THING filter (DoomEd 2014/2015, `options & 2`, not `options & 16`)
  exists in both `world_pickups.rs` and `diagnostic.rs`; a future maintenance pass
  should share one definition.
- Independent review (a separate reviewer that read only the `crates/` diff from
  `ab3f300` and the original `p_inter.c`, `p_mobj.c` and `info.c`) found no
  verified divergence across bonus caps and body sync, armor type, always-collect,
  COUNTITEM order, spawn totals, animation loops, unchanged ordinary grants,
  green armor above 100, legacy policies and rollback. It raised three unconfirmed
  notes: the counted-THING recount assumes medium skill (true for the current
  constructor); pickup rollback relies on the staged plan (confirmed for the tick
  path); and two post-increment validations look unreachable. No code changed.
