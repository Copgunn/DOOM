## Implemented gameplay boundary

PlayerDemonAcquisitionV1 composes one genuine MT_PLAYER=0 and at most one map-THING
MT_SERGEANT=12. Its context fixes source playeringame to `[true,false,false,false]`.
The existing OriginalPlayer and actor store remain authoritative; each scan derives
current health/mo from the transaction's player record. There is no second roster
health cache, actor owner, scheduler, RNG system or player simulation. Source slot0
is independent of the body actor slot. The historical fixture array/API remains
isolated and cannot register/deactivate this player.

Existing turning/thrust/reaction commands execute before the same actor thinker
pass. Source A_Look/scan assign the actual current body handle, emit the single
Demon wake sound and immediately enter original see/Chase chronology. Shared
pursuit preserves direction, movement, collision, threshold, reaction, sight and
RNG. Original SARG ordinals remain stored directly. The new source observations
include the initially failing stop-slot scan, rear62/64/65 boundaries, actually
blocked sight, the inherited horizontal zero-axis sight quirk, moving targets,
controlled knockback, existing targets, target clearing/reacquisition and death.

An active dead player remains listed, allocated and owned but health-ineligible.
Failed Chase fallback/spawn entry retains the dead target handle. This capability
rejects removal of its fixed player's body; deactivation/rebinding/respawn is not
admitted. Demon/effect retirement still uses existing deferred removal and slot
generations. Damage can also retain the player mobj target separately from player.attacker;
neither field is roster activity or a second owner. Retired source identities are
retained without resolving to a later Puff occupying that slot. Foreign provenance remains guarded by existing scopes.

Autonomous melee is not admitted. The old late-melee planner assumes a synthetic
unbound body and does not establish the genuine player's physical damage/death
closure. SARG attack states reject; reaching the actual source melee-selection
branch rejects the complete transaction before attack sound/state/damage commit.
The engine does not silently continue chase through that branch. The existing
controlled physical-damage service remains usable. Actor/geometry capacities,
public historical policies and all older diagnostic meanings are preserved.

## Remaining gameplay and maintenance boundary

The next narrowly scoped gameplay milestone should admit the checked autonomous
Demon-to-player melee DamagePlan closure: source/inflictor, physical impulse/travel,
player/mobj health, pain/death/frags, late RNG/event order and whole-operation
rollback. It should reuse shared combat and scheduling rather than redesign
identity infrastructure. Full player think/death-camera, weapons, inventory,
armor/powers/cheats, roster changes, respawn/multiplayer, generalized infighting,
other monsters, sound propagation, dynamic sectors and rendering remain excluded.

Remaining maintenance priorities retain measurable acceptance criteria: inventory
public-input panic paths and prove checked atomic rejection; name source flags;
measure uniquely useful evidence growth before another trace family; remove one
proven redundant preflight or reuse bounded scratch only with unchanged full-field,
RNG/event and rollback evidence plus matched timings/allocations. No broad cleanup,
framework, actor-capacity expansion or private/incompatible code is included.
