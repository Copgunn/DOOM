## Safety and stepping

Focused Rust policies verify invalid setup/ownership/weapon fields, unsupported
buttons and slots, stale/reused/wrong-definition bodies and foreign instances.
A deliberately exhausted effect capacity fails after earlier shotgun pellets
have already changed staged health. Separate failures after all seven pellets,
a lethal burst with kill credit/corpse fields, and immediate chaingun fire before
noise discard the entire transaction. Full snapshots prove preservation of ammo,
psprites, health, references, RNG, statistics, spatial/thinker order, events and tic.
Malformed DSIM24 fields reject; historical readers/policies keep their meanings.

Successful, dead, paused and rejected calls compare step against advance followed
by explicit snapshot. Structural capture counters verify advance creates no
implicit Snapshot. This does not imply allocation-free gameplay.

## Evidence storage

New reference data occupies29 files /1,892,997 bytes. DREFLZ1 stores the4,957,804-byte
C trace in1,372,656 bytes and the2,031,665-byte independent event stream in258,939
bytes. Fifteen uniquely useful complete fixtures remain directly inspectable.
The bounded standard-library Python codec and safe test-only Rust decoder share
explicit literal/overlap vectors and malformed-input checks. This storage choice
is confined to new test evidence; no historical data migration, dependency, LFS
or external archive was introduced. Packed/unpacked digests and regeneration
preserve source provenance. Different scenario families are not normalized
performance or coverage-density comparisons.
