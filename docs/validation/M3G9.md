original world item admission/contact/removal, bullet/shell supplies, health and
source-gated shotgun/chaingun acquisition. The [support contract](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-world-pickups)

## Integration and gameplay

Separate implementation checkpoints validated ammo, health and world weapon
acquisition. Original definitions/states remain in the same actor store; source
THING order, strict overlap, things-before-lines touch and deferred retirement
reuse the existing movement/spatial/thinker services. Source health/mo and current
ready-dependent pending-weapon rules preserve the same player and inventory.
No alternate combat loop, actor manager, identity field, RNG or gameplay framework
is introduced. DSIM25 carries new persistent bonus/item/message observations;
every earlier schema and no-pickup policy retains its contract.

The normal50-bullet sustained pistol route fires three initial shots, collects a
Clip during real movement, receives genuine Demon melee at254 and heals92→100
from its world medikit in the same source XY pass. Fixed commands resume firing
at255; a later melee strike leaves health64, and actual weapon damage kills the
Demon at441. The losing fixed-command route retains its supplies, later dies
at466 and continues its allocated corpse through512. No adaptive Rust controller,
manually injected combat damage, infinite ammo or changed RNG table supplies
these outcomes. Controlled initial damaged-health and dropped-item cases remain
separate evidence. Shotgun and chaingun items cause real source pending selection,
lowering/raising and firing in the same world; an already-owned full weapon
refuses while new ownership with full ammo still collects.

The final-run wrapper initially reused scratch pre-merge log destinations for
its first four local checks and first19 source commands. Remaining scratch logs
were retained and subsequent results isolated under final-run paths. Readiness,
source prototypes, raw before measurements and every tracked historical evidence
byte remain intact. Overwritten scratch logs are not claimed as retained; the
merge gate also has independently inspected exact-master GitHub CI evidence.
