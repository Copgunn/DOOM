original world item admission/contact/removal, bullet/shell supplies, health and

GitHub. This record separates completed observations from pending final gates.

## Integration and gameplay

Separate implementation checkpoints validated ammo, health and world weapon
acquisition. Original definitions/states remain in the same actor store; source
THING order, strict overlap, things-before-lines touch and deferred retirement
reuse the existing movement/spatial/thinker services. Source health/mo and current
ready-dependent pending-weapon rules preserve the same player and inventory.
No alternate combat loop, actor manager, identity field, RNG or gameplay framework
is introduced. DSIM25 carries new persistent bonus/item/message observations;
every earlier schema and no-pickup policy retains its contract.

The normal50-bullet sustained pistol route fires three initial shots, collects a
Clip during real movement, receives genuine Demon melee at254 and heals92→100
from its world medikit in the same source XY pass. Fixed commands resume firing
at255; a later melee strike leaves health64, and actual weapon damage kills the
Demon at441. The losing fixed-command route retains its supplies, later dies
at466 and continues its allocated corpse through512. No adaptive Rust controller,
manually injected combat damage, infinite ammo or changed RNG table supplies
these outcomes. Controlled initial damaged-health and dropped-item cases remain
separate evidence. Shotgun and chaingun items cause real source pending selection,
lowering/raising and firing in the same world; an already-owned full weapon
refuses while new ownership with full ammo still collects.

The final-run wrapper initially reused scratch pre-merge log destinations for
its first four local checks and first19 source commands. Remaining scratch logs
were retained and subsequent results isolated under final-run paths. Readiness,
source prototypes, raw before measurements and every tracked historical evidence
byte remain intact. Overwritten scratch logs are not claimed as retained; the
merge gate also has independently inspected exact-master GitHub CI evidence.

## Fuzzing and sanitizer observations

All35 distinct address-sanitized fuzz executables were built, then rebuilt at the
final runtime checkpoint. Nine targets ran in10 bounded campaigns totaling40,960
executions without findings/artifacts: world pickups, historical weapon pack,
pistol, melee, commands, knockback, game instance, stepping equivalence and
historical diagnostics. World pickups ran4,096 initial executions, then another
4,096 against the final51-case inputs and checked ammo arithmetic. The final
campaign had1,038 initial seeds, seed400 and162-byte input budget. Every campaign
used a15-second per-input timeout and1,024MiB RSS limit. Other seeds, byte budgets,
executions, elapsed observations and binary/log hashes are in the
[fuzz results](m3g9-safety/fuzz-results.json),
[initial build](m3g9-safety/fuzz-first-build.json),
[final build](m3g9-safety/fuzz-final-build.json) and
[log excerpts](m3g9-safety/fuzz-log-excerpts.txt).

C strict-arithmetic and address/undefined-sanitized51-case observations matched
normal output exactly. All address campaigns used
`ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`; Leak detection was disabled under the established tracing limitation; no
LeakSanitizer campaign ran, so there is no leak-detection coverage. Bounded campaigns do not
prove exhaustive safety, branch coverage or whole-game equivalence. Full raw
logs, prototypes and measurements remain in the workspace; compact hashes and
useful summaries are retained here to avoid duplicating large traces.

## Performance and memory observations

[All raw samples and methods](m3g9-performance/README.md) retain16 matched tools,
247 workload groups, three timing/RSS processes and separate allocation samples.
All207 available workload digests and three-sample allocation arrays match;
45 transaction allocation/copy observations also match. Both batches used the
same Rust1.99.1 toolchain and frozen workload/tool hashes, with no overlapping
local tests, source generation or fuzzing. This is not an allocation-free claim.

Representative production medians, in ms/100 advances: small Profile20
11.002→10.635; four owners46.985→46.390; typed high-grid11.962→11.884, whose
whole-process RSS medians were4,400→4,352KiB. These small shared-host changes
are observations rather than universal gains. Full-batch outliers included
+56.9% pursuit diagnostic-step,+35.5% body demon-source diagnostic-step and
+32.7% continuous-command advance. A quiet three-process interleaved exact-baseline/
current follow-up returned−1.4%,−1.1%,+1.8%; the large magnitudes did not reproduce.
The original outliers and follow-up samples remain visible; three samples do not
establish statistical equivalence. Separate worktree build paths/layout and
uncontrolled host scheduling remain limitations.

New pickup production medians include Clip1.044, sixteen items/slot reuse1.297,
world shotgun6.286, world chaingun6.245 and the sustained winning encounter5.696
ms/100 advances. That encounter measures all512 actual advances and records
848,691 allocations,1,733 reallocations and37,162,144 cumulative requested bytes
for the entire interval. New diagnostic step modes are reported separately.
New workload RSS is roughly35MiB including source-evidence parsing/setup, so it
must not be compared to a bare simulation's RSS as if the workloads were equal.
Allocation requests are cumulative, not peak live heap or leak coverage.

## Preservation, documentation and remaining scope

All165 original upstream files and6,356 protected historical evidence/tool/archive
paths remain byte-identical; the20 exact documentation archives remain verified.
Public visibility and GPL-2.0-only workspace licensing/attribution remain intact.
Both asset-gated tests remain ignored. No private engine source, commercial IWAD,
new unsafe Rust, unrelated dependency/framework, capacity expansion, force-push,
history rewrite or unrelated branch deletion is introduced.

Human-facing support, architecture, identity, DSIM, testing, roadmap and evidence
indexes are updated in their authoritative homes. Link/anchor/archive checks pass.
The feature stops at owner review: exact final-head CI and review state belong to
[GitHub](https://github.com/Copgunn/DOOM/pulls), where Linux, Windows, native Intel
macOS and Rust1.85 are required before readiness. The new PR must remain open,
unmerged and without auto-merge.

Unsupported behavior remains explicit in the [support contract](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-world-pickups):
full player/death thinkers, HUD/audio playback, armor/powers/keys/backpacks, other
weapons/monsters, generalized loot/sound/dynamic geometry, respawn and multiplayer.
Inactive source item-respawn queue storage is outside the projection. The next
recommended gameplay slice is bounded green/blue armor pickup and source damage
absorption through these same player/contact/combat services, with independent C
evidence; it is not admitted here. Existing panic/preflight/allocation/evidence
maintenance priorities remain separately measurable roadmap work.
