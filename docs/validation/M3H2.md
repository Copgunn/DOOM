### The M3h1 bonus `sixteen` regression

M3h1 recorded +9–11% medians with equal minimums and could not separate that from
noise. Measured again here, alone, across `7122e40` and `d997cf9`:

| Measure | 7122e40 | d997cf9 | Δ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 101 interleaved pairs, median ms | 1.788 | 1.889 | +5.6% |
| 101 interleaved pairs, minimum ms | 1.713 | 1.803 | +5.3% |
| Instructions over a 20,000-tic scratch run | 4.865 G | 5.101 G | +4.8% |

**It is a real, repeatable cost, not host noise,** and the earlier "equal minimums"
observation did not reproduce. Instruction sampling over the lengthened run
(two runs each, about 17,000 samples per build) attributes it: +583 of the +920 extra
samples land in the sound-array closure of `OriginalActorType::descriptor`, which
exists as a separate symbol only in `d997cf9`, because that closure stopped being
inlined. `world_pickups::validate_definition` reaches `descriptor` for every item on
every tic's preflight. Smaller additions are in `monster_actor`, `validate_monster_type`
and `preflight_map_demons`. About 40% of samples in both builds are per-tic registry
revalidation, which is the larger opportunity. The lengthened run was a scratch edit
and is not committed. M3h2 made no fix; this head measures -1.8% against `d997cf9`.

## Independent review

A fresh reviewer read only the `crates/` diff from `d997cf9` and the original source,
without running anything. It reported no reachable panic, no source-order or RNG
divergence and no historical regression, and two verified low-severity bugs, both
fixed in `428f903` with regression tests:

- The DSIM29 live-fireball bound relied on field order, which the reader does not
  enforce. It now reads each slot.
- `check_multisector_position` answered a fireball with the non-missile rule, so a new
  fireball appeared blocked by its owner. It now uses the missile contact rule.

Its unproven suspicions stay open as limits:

- The 16-fireball cap has no proof against play. Flight time grows with room size, and
  a large room could reach the cap, after which that tic keeps rejecting. The comment
  and support text claimed more than this and were corrected. No soak test ran.
- Admission permits walls beyond the 994-unit actor bound, so a fireball that crosses
  it rejects later tics. The class predates M3h2, but a monster can now trigger it
  without player action.
- The visit budget equals the slot limit. A near-full store that retires and refills a
  slot now rejects instead of panicking, and that tic repeats. Whether 15 impact
  slots always suffice is unproven.
