## The two M3h2 stalls

Committed tests (`stall_*` in `player_map_scale_tests.rs`) on the historical policies,
all with the complete state unchanged by the rejected tic and the same rejection on 64
further zero-command tics:

| Scenario | Policy | Observed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fireball hit on a player 16 units from the wall | PlayerMonstersImpV1 | Launch at tic 16, `PlayerBodyError::Unsupported` at tic 31, permanent |
| Point-blank shotgun volley, no Imp | PlayerMonstersV1 (base master) | `PlayerBodyError::Unsupported` on the volley tic, permanent |
| Missed fireball flying east past 994 | PlayerMonstersImpV1 | `MonsterError::Arithmetic` at tic 216 with the fireball at x 1,002, permanent |

The "fireballs past 994" claim is therefore **confirmed**, not refuted. The same three
scenarios run to completion under `PlayerMonstersMapScaleV1` (`map_scale_*` tests) and
match the compiled C rows field for field (scenarios 0–2 below).

## Independent review

A fresh reviewer read only the `crates/` diff from `2da111d` and the original
`p_mobj.c`, `p_map.c`, `p_maputl.c`, `p_enemy.c`, `p_inter.c`, `p_sight.c`,
`p_setup.c`, `r_main.c`, `m_fixed.c` and `info.c`, without running anything. It
confirmed that every historical policy keeps its bounds, errors and digests, traced
the wall invariant through the Rust movers (clamp, half-step split, slide, stairstep,
fireball half-step and explosion, puffs, blood, drops) and the intercept arithmetic at
`G = A = 2048`, checked the memo's inputs for mutability and the DSIM30 reader's
rejection of relabeled and over-sized frames, and found no false-positive path in the
fuzz progress property. It reported two verified issues, both fixed in `91bbb04` with
regression tests:

- `RoomBounds::contains_box` formed its box edges with wrapping fixed-point
  arithmetic, so a THING or body within one radius of the coordinate minimum passed
  the spawn test and then stalled every tic (medium). The edges are now formed in i64.
- The Imp's minimum launch interval is 23 tics, not 25: `P_DamageMobj` cuts the attack
  state short through `S_TROO_PAIN`, and `P_CheckMissileRange` then passes on
  `MF_JUSTHIT`. One Imp can hold 26 fireballs in the largest room, so the derived
  bounds became 208 live fireballs and 320 slots, with the slot arithmetic keeping a
  64-visit margin under the thinker budget (low in practice, but the derivation is the
  stated proof). A test now derives both numbers from the source state tables.

Its suspicions were checked: `Simulation` is no longer `Sync` because of the
`Cell<[u64; 4]>` memo (nothing in this repository shares a simulation across threads;
noted as an API observation); the static `validate_definition` memo also retains an
`Err` for a type for the process lifetime, which is sound because its inputs are
compile-time tables; the one-unit coverage rule in `RoomBounds::derive` is
conservative, not unsound; and the soak and fuzz runs cannot approach the derived caps,
so those rest on the derivation and its test.

## Open limits and follow-ups

- Non-axis walls stay excluded: with `P_AproxDistance(30, 30) = 45`, a 22.5° slide can
  move a player 38 units, more than its 32-unit box, so the wall argument needs its
  own proof before such rooms are admitted.
- The 4,096-unit momentum bound is reachable only by repeated controlled
  `damage_actor` calls between tics; the rejection rolls back and is not a stall of
  ordinary play.
- Puffs and blood near walls carry centers inside the rectangle but boxes across the
  wall; they never move in XY, so the invariant is stated for movers only.
- The historical fuzz targets (and the new one, written in the same style) trip two
  stable-Clippy lints that are new in rustc 1.99 (`chunks_exact` with a constant size,
  manual `is_multiple_of`); the fuzz workspace is not a CI Clippy gate and the lints
  are present on `master` too, so they were left alone.
- Two-sided lines and height changes remain the recommended next milestone, with the
  wall invariant extended to openings.
