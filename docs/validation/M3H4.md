## Baseline and host

The branch starts from the PR45 merge `d9ad98f` (M3h3). Historical evidence inputs,
fixtures, zstd identities and DSIM20–30 expectations are unchanged.

Ran locally in WSL2 Ubuntu: Intel Core i7-8750H @ 2.20GHz, 6 cores/12 threads, 9 GiB
RAM visible to WSL; rustc 1.99.1 stable, 1.85.0 and nightly 1.101.0 (2026-10-31);
cargo-fuzz 0.13.2; gcc 15.2.0; clang 21.1.8; Python 3.14.4. `DOOM_IWAD_DIR` was unset,
so the two asset-gated tests stayed ignored.

## Rust source replay

projection with no change to the movement, collision, sight, hitscan, missile or noise
bodies: the engine's M3b-era implementations of `P_LineOpening`, `PIT_CheckLine`,
`P_TryMove`, `P_SlideMove`, `P_ZMovement`, `P_Move`, `P_CrossSubsector`,

## Rust-only policy tests

`player_heights_tests` (33 tests):

- The test-only room builder reproduces five committed rooms byte for byte (stairs,
  window, sound-blocking window, REJECT window, fence).
- Load-time rejections with the stated errors: a diagonal two-sided line, a forbidden
  flag bit on a one-sided wall and on a two-sided line, an inconsistent two-sided pair
  (`MapScaleError::Geometry`); a two-sided boundary line without a one-sided wall
  (`Walls`); a two-sided line listed in no BLOCKMAP cell (`Blockmap`); a line special
  and a sector special (`PursuitError::Specials`); an `F_SKY1` ceiling
  (`CombatError::Sky`); a 513-unit ceiling (`PerceptionError::Arithmetic`); an inverted
  sector; 121 linedefs (`Lines`); the map-scale policy refusing every heights room
  (`PlayerBodyError::Unsupported`); a 64-slot construction (`StoreError::InvalidLimit`).
  Every admitted flag bit loads on both line kinds, and a closed-door sector loads as a
  static wall.
- Spawn fit: a THING in the 48-unit window and in a closed sector, the player body in
  the 32-unit crawlspace and a dropped pickup in a closed sector reject with
  `MapScaleError::Fit` and unchanged state; the same pickup fits the window.
- Witnesses: a monster above its ceiling, below its floor, or with `momz` beyond
  ±1,024 rejects the tic with `MapScaleError::Vertical`, state and RNG unchanged; a
  monster lifted 48 units above its floor is admitted and falls back in 20 tics.
- A fireball chord with `momz` 121 units per tic (an Imp 968 units below a player 80
  units away) is admitted and the world progresses.
- Wire 15, the heights context and `has_map_scale()` hold; older policies do not
  report heights.
- DSIM31: `decode_heights` accepts a heights frame that `decode_map_scale`,
  `decode_imps`, `decode_monsters` and `decode` reject; 321 slots, policy 14, an item
  floor of 0 or ceiling of 128 in a sector of 24/256 and a frame relabeled 30 are
  rejected; a map-scale frame is not a heights frame.
- Soaks: the stairs and the ledge rooms with 16 items, five Imps and three former
  humans, a running, strafing, firing chaingun player (zero commands once dead), four
  seeds, 1,200 tics each, no rejection, the vertical invariant holding for every
  non-impact actor after every tic, and falling observed in every run.
