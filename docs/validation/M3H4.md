This record owns observed engineering results. [Compatibility](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-monsters-with-static-heights)

## Baseline and host

The branch starts from the PR45 merge `d9ad98f` (M3h3). Historical evidence inputs,

RAM visible to WSL; rustc 1.99.1 stable, 1.85.0 and nightly 1.101.0 (2026-10-31);

so the two asset-gated tests stayed ignored.

## Rust-only policy tests

`player_heights_tests` (33 tests):

- The test-only room builder reproduces five committed rooms byte for byte (stairs,
  window, sound-blocking window, REJECT window, fence).
- Load-time rejections with the stated errors: a diagonal two-sided line, a forbidden
  flag bit on a one-sided wall and on a two-sided line, an inconsistent two-sided pair
  (`MapScaleError::Geometry`); a two-sided boundary line without a one-sided wall
  (`Walls`); a two-sided line listed in no BLOCKMAP cell (`Blockmap`); a line special
  and a sector special (`PursuitError::Specials`); an `F_SKY1` ceiling
  (`CombatError::Sky`); a 513-unit ceiling (`PerceptionError::Arithmetic`); an inverted
  sector; 121 linedefs (`Lines`); the map-scale policy refusing every heights room
  (`PlayerBodyError::Unsupported`); a 64-slot construction (`StoreError::InvalidLimit`).
  Every admitted flag bit loads on both line kinds, and a closed-door sector loads as a
  static wall.
- Spawn fit: a THING in the 48-unit window and in a closed sector, the player body in
  the 32-unit crawlspace and a dropped pickup in a closed sector reject with
  `MapScaleError::Fit` and unchanged state; the same pickup fits the window.
- Witnesses: a monster above its ceiling, below its floor, or with `momz` beyond
  ±1,024 rejects the tic with `MapScaleError::Vertical`, state and RNG unchanged; a
  monster lifted 48 units above its floor is admitted and falls back in 20 tics.
- A fireball chord with `momz` 121 units per tic (an Imp 968 units below a player 80
  units away) is admitted and the world progresses.
- Wire 15, the heights context and `has_map_scale()` hold; older policies do not
  report heights.
- DSIM31: `decode_heights` accepts a heights frame that `decode_map_scale`,
  `decode_imps`, `decode_monsters` and `decode` reject; 321 slots, policy 14, an item
  floor of 0 or ceiling of 128 in a sector of 24/256 and a frame relabeled 30 are
  rejected; a map-scale frame is not a heights frame.
- Soaks: the stairs and the ledge rooms with 16 items, five Imps and three former
  humans, a running, strafing, firing chaingun player (zero commands once dead), four
  seeds, 1,200 tics each, no rejection, the vertical invariant holding for every
  non-impact actor after every tic, and falling observed in every run.

## Independent review

A fresh reviewer with no access to this branch's reasoning read the full diff from
`d9ad98f` against `p_mobj.c`, `p_map.c`, `p_maputl.c`, `p_enemy.c`, `p_inter.c`,
`p_sight.c`, `p_setup.c`, `r_main.c`, `sounds.h` and `doomdata.h`, the Python harness
and the documentation, without running anything. It verified the landing sound
(threshold, sound index, actor, event order), the noise flood line for line against
`P_RecursiveSound` and `Simulation::emit_noise`, every `has_heights` gate's unchanged
`false` arm, the DSIM31 range widening, the admission checks, the BSP side convention
of the room builders and every number quoted in this record against the committed
contract. It confirmed no bug and reported three risks and three documentation nits,
all addressed in `13464eb`:

- The vertical-invariant proof and tests did not cover `P_CheckMissileSpawn`'s
  half-step: a steep chord can explode a fireball in place below its floor or above
  its ceiling, and V is restored only by the new mobj's own `P_ZMovement` in the same
  pass. The proof sketch now states the case; the runtime witness exempts an exploded,
  not yet relinked fireball (as the location check already did for its XY); a test
  launches a `momz` −166 chord both between tics and inside a tic and observes the
  clip.
- The DSIM31 item witness scanned every sector list for the item's handle and ignored
  the per-actor sector field; it now reads `actor.{slot}.sector`, requires that
  sector's list to hold the handle and compares that sector's heights.
- `spawn_type` ran the fit lookup (a BSP descent) before the position bound; the order
  is now bound first, then fit, matching the THING batch and dropped pickups.
- Documentation: the measured workload list in the note, the room count wording in
  this record, and the nominal 256-sector bound (in practice about 30 sectors under
  the 95-linedef cap).

## Open limits and follow-ups

- Non-axis walls stay excluded; they need their own slide proof before diagonal lines
  (and real E1M1 geometry) are admitted. Nothing in this milestone's evidence changed
  that assessment.
- Moving sectors, doors, lifts, crushers, specials, teleporters, damaging floors and
  sky ceilings are rejected at load; the recommended next milestone after non-axis
  walls is doors and lifts as the first specials.
- The `P_KillMobj` drop fit check is unreachable for the admitted roster (a dying
  monster's sector is at least as tall as the monster, hence taller than a 16-unit
  item); it fails the checked tic if ever reached, and no test reaches it.
- The spawn fit, the type dimension envelope and the vertical witnesses are Rust-only
  policy; the source would stand a too-tall thing through its ceiling.
- The autoaim trace's block-row drift at exactly 180° (`autoaim_down_step`) is source
  behavior the Rust trace already reproduces; it is recorded in the note as an
  observation, not as a geometry claim.
