This record owns observed engineering results. [Compatibility](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-monsters-among-diagonal-walls)
owns the executable domain; [investigation](../M3H5_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md)
owns source facts, the admitted domain, the proof and its hole; [format](../STATE_FORMAT.md#dsim-v32-original-monsters-among-diagonal-walls)
owns DSIM32. Exact review state belongs to GitHub. No full-player, whole-ticker,
DOS-demo or whole-game equivalence is claimed.

## Baseline and host

The branch starts from the PR48 merge `5e711be` (M3h4 plus its review fixes).
Historical evidence inputs, fixtures, zstd identities and DSIM20–31 expectations are
unchanged.

Ran locally in WSL2 Ubuntu: Intel Core i7-8750H @ 2.20GHz, 6 cores/12 threads, 9 GiB
RAM visible to WSL; rustc stable, 1.85.0 and nightly 1.101.0 (2026-10-27);
cargo-fuzz 0.13.2; Python 3. `DOOM_IWAD_DIR` was unset, so the asset-gated tests
stayed ignored.

## Rust source replay

Every committed scenario replays against the compiled rows and the independent DSIM32
projection with no change to the movement, collision, traversal, sight, hitscan,
missile, noise or pursuit bodies: the angle-general implementations the engine already
carried matched the C rows once the admission admitted the maps. Two things outside the
runtime needed correction during Phase 2: the DSIM32 projection had to derive each
actor's sector from its subsector's first seg (every earlier builder had one subsector
per sector, so the frozen layers wrote the subsector index into `actor.N.sector`), and
the Rust mirror of the harness push did not exist (`set_momentum` refused the player
body under every physical-player policy), so it admits the player body under this
policy only.

## Rust-only policy tests

`player_diagonal_tests` (32 tests):

- Every committed map loads under the policy with every case's spawns admitted; the
  heights policy rejects each of them at its axis rule (`MapScaleError::Geometry`) and
  the map-scale policy at its grounded-geometry rule (`PlayerBodyError::Unsupported`).
- Load-time rejections built by patching the committed lumps, each with the stated
  error: a reversed 45° wall (C3), a wall shortened to a new end (C2), the tip vertex
  moved so both tip walls cross the horizontal wall (C1), the pillar's north face
  stretched over the room's top wall (collinear overlap, C1), all
  `MapScaleError::Boundary`; a degenerate line, a forbidden flag bit on a diagonal
  wall, a diagonal two-sided line without `ML_TWOSIDED` (`Geometry`); a diagonal wall
  and a diagonal corridor wall piece listed in no BLOCKMAP cell (`Blockmap`); a line
  special and a sector special (`PursuitError::Specials`); an `F_SKY1` ceiling
  (`CombatError::Sky`); a ceiling at 513 (`PerceptionError::Arithmetic`). Every
  admitted flag bit loads on a diagonal wall and on a diagonal two-sided line.
  Unlisting line 0 changes nothing: every cell's leading 0 lists it, as the source's
  `P_BlockLinesIterator` reads the list from its first word.
- Spawns whose closed box touches the 45° wall, whose center is in the void beyond it
  or outside the vertex rectangle are rejected before any spawn with unchanged state,
  as THINGs (`MonsterError::Arithmetic`) and as the player body
  (`PlayerBodyError::Binding`); the committed `slide_45` start 20 units from the wall
  is admitted.
- The sliver quirks on a `(1, 1)` wall through the origin: a point on the line reads
  back, a point 0.5 unit² in front reads back, a point 2 unit² in front reads front; a
  box with its corner exactly on the wall is read as crossing from the front side and
  as one side from the back.
- `Boundary::inside` and `box_meets_wall` are exact: one raw unit across the 5:1 wall
  flips the parity, a box touching the wall by one raw unit meets it.
- The escape witness is recoverable: the `(30, 30)` push at `(−31, −20)` in the
  committed corner room fails the tic with `Escape`, the snapshot and event log are
  unchanged, and the same start under a `(24, 24)` push advances with the center
  inside; the heights policy refuses the push with `PlayerBodyError::Unsupported`.
- Policy identity: wire 16, the diagonal context, `has_heights`/`has_diagonals`/
  `has_map_scale`; `decode_diagonal` reads the policy's bytes and `decode_heights` and
  `decode_map_scale` reject them; a DSIM31 capture is rejected by `decode_diagonal`.
- Soaks: the wedge and the pillar room with the full eight-monster roster and a
  firing, running player for 1,200 tics on four seeds each, asserting Invariant Ω
  (every live non-item actor's center inside the boundary with its open box meeting no
  wall, every actor inside the rectangle) after every tic.

## Independent review

An independent reviewer subagent with no access to the implementation reasoning read
the full diff against the preserved C sources and the investigation note (read-only;
it ran the 32 policy tests and nothing heavier). It found no defect in source
fidelity, in the admission arithmetic (it re-derived the C3 tie rule and the box test
and tried backwards, dangling, crossing and overlapping boundaries without finding a
wrong accept), in the DSIM32 ranges or in the harness/adapter agreement. Findings and
what was done:

1. High, `run_reference.py`: an exception inside a worker thread (a failed workspace
   copy, an unrunnable command) left `results` holes and still printed the pass line
   with exit 0. Fixed: worker exceptions are recorded and re-raised, the run fails if
   any command did not run, and every command's own output is printed again (the
   sequential run had streamed it).
2. Medium: the duration table did not exist while the docs described it as committed,
   and the CI switch to `--jobs 4` preceded the owner-required serial/parallel
   comparison. Fixed by sequencing: the table and the CI switch are committed together
   with the comparison recorded below.
3. Medium, `COMPATIBILITY.md`: the spawn row claimed `P_KillMobj` drops get the
   parity-and-closed-box rule; the code applies only the heights fit (a drop spawns
   inside the corpse's box, and a corpse may legally stand with its box edge exactly on
   an axis wall, so the documented rule would have failed a legal kill). The doc was
   corrected; the code was right.
4. Low: `set_momentum` under the policy admits every non-fireball actor, not only the
   player body as documented. Fireballs stay refused by the projectile control, so every
   pushed mover is one the clamp covers; the docs now say so.
5. Low: `--jobs 1` had stopped streaming command output and the report embedded whole
   outputs. Fixed with finding 1 (outputs printed, the report keeps the digest).
6. Low: the note described C3 with a vertical ray for horizontal walls; the code uses
   the horizontal ray with a tie rule for every wall. The note now describes the code.
7. Low: the "sub-unit penetration from the back" quirk was implied by the point-side
   assertion, not asserted with a box. A box assertion was added (a leading corner
   0.25 unit² past the `(1, 1)` wall reads one side; 1 unit² reads crossing).
8. Low: the `inside` doc comment overstated what a center exactly on a wall yields.
   Reworded; no caller relies on it.

## Open limits and follow-ups

- The corner jump is the one legal source move the policy refuses (a known fidelity
  gap to revisit for M4 demo compatibility); the proof covers every move of at most
  `2r − 2` units per axis, and a projected slide above 30 units per axis that stays
  inside the room is admitted.
- Boundary rule C3 (walls facing their room) and the closed-cell BLOCKMAP rule are
  stricter than real maps: real-map loading must revisit backwards or dangling
  one-sided lines and BLOCKMAPs from real node builders.
- The line, sector and coordinate caps are unchanged; raising them is the next
  milestone.
