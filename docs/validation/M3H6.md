# M3h6 — Real-map scale: classic map limits toward E1M1

This record owns observed engineering results. [Compatibility](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-monsters-at-classic-map-scale)
owns the executable domain; [investigation](../M3H6_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md)
owns source facts, the cap inventory, the certificate and the plan; [format](../STATE_FORMAT.md#dsim-v33-original-monsters-at-classic-map-scale)
owns DSIM33; [IWAD counts](M3H6_IWAD_COUNTS.md) owns the per-map pass/fail table.
Exact review state belongs to GitHub. No full-player, whole-ticker, DOS-demo or
whole-game equivalence is claimed, and E1M1 itself is not admitted (its THINGs, sky and
specials stay rejected; see the readiness report of the investigation).

## Baseline and host

The branch starts from master `de042dd` (the PR 49 merge of M3h5). Historical evidence
inputs, fixtures and DSIM20–32 expectations are unchanged.

Ran locally in WSL2 Ubuntu (kernel 6.18.54.1): Intel Core i7-8750H @ 2.20GHz, 6
cores/12 threads, 9 GiB RAM visible to WSL; rustc stable 1.99.1, 1.85.0 and nightly
1.101.0 (2026-10-27); cargo-fuzz 0.13.2; gcc 15.2.0; clang 21.1.8; Python 3.14.4. The
owner's IWADs at `/mnt/d/DoomIWADs` were read locally and read-only for the counts
below and the opt-in test; nothing derived from them beyond counts and pass/fail facts
is committed.

loader or node builder; function-entry counts show entered bodies, not branch coverage.
The harness keeps `intercept_t intercepts[128]` and the abort guard before its 129th
entry exactly, so every trace observed holds fewer than 128 intercepts and an overflow
would have aborted the run: no source overflow of `MAXINTERCEPTS` is observed or claimed.
Per scenario (`--cases`): `town_patrol` walks five corridors east while a Demon in the
last room pursues (353 blocked moves, 35 sight queries, 1 kill: the Demon reaches the
player); `town_fight` runs the full roster and sixteen pickups for 420 tics (98

Two Phase 2 corrections to the Phase 1 plan are recorded in the investigation note: the
extent room was redesigned (no wall near the (i) limit can share a map with a span near
it, because (ii) bounds `diag · L₂`), and the gallery's teeth became two-sided sectors
(one-sided teeth block the sight line of any actor aiming along the band, so no monster
could fire the long trace the scenario was meant to observe).

## Rust source replay

`real_map_source_case_00`–`19` replay every committed scenario: source actor, player,
weapon, armor, pickup and statistics fields, ordered DSIM33 events, digests, full bytes
for 9 captures and decode/re-encode round trips, with `step` equal to `advance` at every
tic. No scenario is refused. The example prints `digest=…` for the combined final digest
and asserts the committed row counts (615 slides, 1,508 blocked moves, 136 hitscan line
impacts, 5,693 noise floods, a largest trace of 73 intercepts).
