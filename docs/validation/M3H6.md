inputs, fixtures and DSIM20–32 expectations are unchanged.

counts ran locally in WSL2 Ubuntu (kernel 6.18.54.1): Intel Core i7-8750H @ 2.20GHz, 6

could fire the long trace the scenario was meant to observe).

## Independent review

An independent reviewer subagent with fresh context reviewed the Phase 2 diff locally
before the incident; its numbered findings list was in the lost local progress note.
What it found and what was done is recorded in commit `8316b2b` and reproduced here:

1. DSIM33 armor and pickup events were validated under the wrong policy (the version
   ladder stopped at 32), so a capture with green armor above 100 points after bonuses
   was refused. Fixed in both ladders, with a regression test (a chaingunner hits an
   armored player; every capture decodes).
2. The arithmetic certificate lacked a reach clause. Clause (viii) was added: the
   rectangle stays 2,177 units inside the short range and the BLOCKMAP origin, and every
   node partition origin lies in the rectangle; `ArithmeticCertificate::evaluate`
   reports each clause and the refusal tests assert the clause they claim.
3. The replay harness did not assert the committed capture and tic counts; it now does
   and panics on a missing row.
4. The noise flood allocated on the shared path; it no longer does.
5. The IWAD-gated test now skips when unset and bounds its lump reads;
   `iwad_counts.py` honors `DOOM_IWAD_DIR`.
6. The node builder now asserts non-degenerate split pieces and bounded recursion.
7. Documents corrected: the 64-query sight budget, the IWAD exceptions, the bitset and
   guard descriptions, E1M1 spans instead of coordinates, the departures list.

Commit `ade5cd8` then regenerated the M3h6 contracts for the builder guards and
docstrings. No `/code-review ultra` was run: the diff against master is about 8,600
changed lines, over its limit.

## Local filesystem incident and recovery

After `ade5cd8` was committed, the owner's WSL2 filesystem was corrupted and the local
machine became unusable; the local progress note was lost. The branch had already been
pushed by the owner. A fresh cloud clone of `origin/feat/m3h6-real-map-scale` was at
`ade5cd8`, `git fsck --full` reported no errors (only dangling commits), and the
remaining steps (the gates and timings above, these sections) ran on the cloud host.
No commit was rewritten.
