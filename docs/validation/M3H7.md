# M3h7 — Sky ceilings and static sector specials (Stage A)

This record owns observed engineering results. The
[investigation](../M3H7_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md) owns source facts, the admitted
domain, the proof obligations A1–A7 (§4.2) and the plan; [format](../STATE_FORMAT.md#dsim-v34--original-monsters-with-sky-and-static-specials)
owns DSIM34. Exact review state belongs to GitHub. No full-player, whole-ticker,
DOS-demo or whole-game equivalence is claimed, and E1M1 itself is not admitted.

The observations below ran in a cloud container: a 4-vCPU Intel Xeon @ 2.30GHz, 15 GiB
RAM, Linux 6.18.44; rustc stable 1.99.1 and nightly 1.101.0 (2026-11-02); cargo-fuzz
0.13.2; gcc 13.3.0; clang 18.1.3; Python 3.13.18. The replay, test and fuzzing
sections are correctness observations, not performance measurements, and no timing
in them is a measurement; measured timings appear only under
[matched performance](#matched-performance), with their own conditions.

## Rust source replay

`specials_source_case_00`–`16` replay every committed scenario of the `pools` and `sky`
rooms against the compiled original rows: actor, player, weapon, armor, pickup and
statistics fields, every `SX` (sector light, special, secret counts) and `TK` (shared
thinker order with every light thinker's fields) value, the RNG, the 427 independent
DSIM34 captures, the 9 full-byte fixtures and the ordered events, with `step` equal to
`advance` at every tic and no scenario refused. `specials_source_all_captures_and_tics`
requires 427 captures, 5,177 compared tics and every `SX`/`TK` value compared (400,954).

The `player_specials` example runs the same replay in debug and release and prints the
combined final digest (`digest=d7b2fdbe74d54e6f`, equal in both); it then asserts the
committed headline counts of the C rows (light changes flash/strobe/glow/flicker
156/1,001/1,897/218; 20 null-source environmental damage calls; 1 secret; 8 sky
removals; 4 sky shots above the front ceiling and 4 at a hack wall; 689
`P_PlayerInSpecialSector` entries; 56 light thinkers spawned at load over the 17
scenarios) and that the Rust run emits the same numbers of DSIM34 secret, sky-removal
and sky-shot events. `check_player_specials.py` asserts debug/release equality.

## Fuzzing

Target compiled with `cargo +nightly fuzz build player_specials --sanitizer address`
(libFuzzer, AddressSanitizer). Every run used `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`, so
LeakSanitizer was disabled, not passed. The target admits one of the 17 committed
scenario spawns (11 maps of the `pools` and `sky` rooms), spawns the THINGs, the body
and `spawn_level_specials`, and applies up to 32 nine-byte operations (commands,
damage, retarget, removal, pause, push) with the M3h6 properties (Ω and V after every
operation, `step` equal to `advance`, a refused operation keeps the state, a valid
command refused only by an existing witness, every snapshot decodes through
`decode_specials` and re-encodes to the same bytes, byte flips of three DSIM34 fixtures
and of each capture never panic) plus the light envelopes, the shared thinker order and
`secretcount ≤ totalsecret`.

| Campaign | Observed |
| --- | --- |
| Seed replay: 4,556 seeds from `python3 tools/seed_player_specials_fuzz.py /tmp/specials-seeds` (17 spawns × 4 RNG indices × 67 inputs: idle, firing, running, strafing and turning for 32 operations, turning north or south and walking or running into the basins or toward the sky walls, damage, retarget, removal, pause and push operations of the M3h6 seeds), `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 cargo +nightly fuzz run player_specials <copy of the seeds> --sanitizer address -- -runs=0 -max_len=290 -timeout=15 -rss_limit_mb=1024` | 4,557 runs (the seeds plus libFuzzer's empty input) in 379 s; final stats `cov: 9080 ft: 25181 corp: 534/97Kb`; no crash, timeout, assertion or sanitizer report |
| Coverage-guided campaign: `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 cargo +nightly fuzz run player_specials /tmp/specials-corpus --sanitizer address -- -runs=8653 -seed=501 -max_len=290 -timeout=15 -rss_limit_mb=1024` on a fresh copy of the 4,556 seeds | 8,653 runs in 768 s (769 s wall): the 4,557 initial-corpus runs (`INITED cov: 9080 ft: 25181 corp: 543/101Kb`), then 4,096 mutation runs; final stats `DONE cov: 9239 ft: 30192 corp: 686/127Kb`; 144 new inputs written (4,700 files on disk); no crash, timeout, assertion or sanitizer report. The campaign is bounded and not exhaustive |

A first campaign with `-runs=4096` (the M3h6 bound) ended at `#4557 DONE` without any
mutation run, because libFuzzer counts the initial-corpus runs against `-runs` and the
4,556 seeds exceed 4,096; it is not counted as a coverage-guided campaign. The rerun
above raises the bound by exactly the initial-corpus runs. Every other fuzz target was
built with `cargo +nightly fuzz build --sanitizer address` (45 targets, success); none
was run. The corpora under `/tmp` and `fuzz/target` were deleted after these numbers
were recorded.

After the review's decoder cross-checks (code at `42d88cf`, unchanged since), both runs
were repeated on the cloud host with the same commands and `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`
(LeakSanitizer disabled, not passed): the seed replay of the same 4,556 seeds took 4,557
runs in 387 s (`cov: 9107 ft: 25275 corp: 535/98Kb`), and the coverage-guided campaign
(`-runs=8653 -seed=501`) 8,653 runs in 746 s, from `INITED cov: 9107 ft: 25276 corp:
544/101Kb` to `DONE cov: 9211 ft: 28960 corp: 593/111Kb` (4,605 files on disk); no crash,
timeout, assertion or sanitizer report, so every snapshot the target reached also passes
the stricter `decode_specials`. Corpora and `fuzz/target` were deleted afterwards.

## Independent review

An independent reviewer subagent with fresh context read the full diff against master
and the preserved C sources (read-only; it ran the policy tests, the real-map tests, the
transaction-cost test and the M3h7 generator and goldens checks, nothing heavier). It
found no high-severity issue: it re-checked the four light bodies and spawners
(including `P_Random() & 64`, the strobe `minlight` rule and the type-4 restore),
`P_FindMinSurroundingLight` over the `P_GroupLines` lists, `P_PlayerInSpecialSector`
(sector floor, `leveltime & 31`, the unreachable suit draw, the secret clear), null-source
damage and death, both sky bodies and the `ceilingline` derivation, the thinker and RNG
order, the admission and its rejections, the DSIM34 layout against the specification,
transaction staging and the harness's use of compiled bodies only. Findings and what was
done:

1. Medium, `store.rs`: `ThinkerStore::order()` filtered the shared order through a
   per-entry kind lookup, several times per tic for every policy, the source of the
   positive lean in the first timing run. Fixed in `31bd946` (one slot lookup per entry;
   the instruction counts and the second timing run are under
   [Matched performance](#matched-performance)).
2. Medium, this record: a sentence said no timing here is a measurement, contradicting the
   timing section. Scoped to the correctness sections.
3. Low, investigation §9: the status line was stale and repeated ROADMAP. Replaced by a
   pointer; steps 1 and 2 added to the step log.
4. Low: the documents said the sector-thinker staging allocates nothing once sized, but
   `demon_plan` copied the slots into a fresh vector every tic. A reused buffer
   (`spare_sector_slots`) was added, with a test; a refused tic still drops it, as the
   architecture page now says. Older policies stage an empty vector and allocate nothing.
5. Low, `decode_specials`: it checked widths and ranges, not consistency. It now also
   rejects two thinkers on one sector, a thinker whose sector's map special does not spawn
   its kind, a slot count other than the light-sector count after load, a light sector
   with a runtime special other than 0 (4 for type 4), other runtime specials differing
   from the map except a found secret, a sky event naming a line beyond the map, and a
   sky removal whose handle is not a live `MT_TROOPSHOT`; twelve crafted mutations are
   rejected and every committed capture still decodes.
6. Low: the `ML_TWOSIDED` skip of `getNextSector` is unreachable under admission (lines
   with a back side but no flag are rejected at load). Kept for fidelity, now commented.
7. Low, obligation A5: no case had the player above a secret sector's floor. A Rust-only
   test now lowers the secret basin: no secret while the body is above the floor, exactly
   one on landing.
8. Low, COMPATIBILITY: "P_CalcHeight / view bobbing excluded" overstated the new section;
   it now says only the momentum bob is computed and `viewz` stays excluded.
9. Low, `specials_snapshot.py`: a sky-shot row without its end row was appended at the
   end of the stream instead of failing; it is now an assertion. Every generated output
   is byte-identical except the contract's hash of that script.

The per-tic `BTreeSet` of `preflight_expanded_demons_for` is master behavior and was left
unchanged. No `/code-review ultra` or other ultrareview was run: the diff against master
is over its size limit.
