# M3h9 evidence record

Observed results of the M3h9 milestone (`PlayerMonstersE1M1V1`, wire 20, DSIM36), the
first part (M3h9a) of the split recommended in the
[investigation](../M3H9_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md) §8. Only observations appear here.
The investigation owns the source facts, the admitted domain, the budgets (§5.3), the
decisions (§6) and the plan (§9);
[format](../STATE_FORMAT.md#dsim-v36--original-two-sided-scrollers-and-static-decorations)
owns DSIM36 and
[support](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-monsters-with-two-sided-scrolling-walls-and-static-decorations)
the admitted behavior. Exact review state belongs to GitHub. No full-player,
whole-ticker, DOS-demo or whole-game equivalence is claimed, and E1M1 itself does not
load under this policy.

**This record is incomplete.** It holds what the implementing session observed through
plan step 10 (the branch up to `bc06aed`): the C evidence, the independent projection, the Rust
replay, the Rust-only tests and the fuzz target with its seed replay; and the
independent review's findings and their fixes (plan step 12), with the regenerated
evidence. The fuzz campaigns, matched timings, local gates and CI have not been run;
their sections below say so, and nothing in them may be read as a pass. The replay, test and fuzzing
sections are correctness observations, not performance measurements, and no time in them
is a measurement.

## IWAD counts

The per-skill THING census of the owner's two IWADs was read locally and read-only on
2026-11-03 with `tools/iwad_counts.py --things-markdown`, and again on 2026-11-04 after
the review added the angle-octant counts (the six other modes and every earlier line of
the two THING modes were byte-identical before and after); the counts-only output is in
[M3H9_IWAD_COUNTS.md](M3H9_IWAD_COUNTS.md). On skill 3 single player E1M1 spawns 91
things: 6 monsters, 52 pickups, 12 solid and 15 non-solid decorations and 6 barrels; 45
of the 91 have an angle octant of 4..7 (44 on easy, 52 on hard), outside the inherited
`[-4,3]` spawn-angle rule.
No IWAD content enters the repository, CI or logs.

## Original-C evidence

`tools/reference/m3h9_reference.py --check --overflow-check --address-check` (with
`ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`, so LeakSanitizer was disabled, not passed) reuses every
compiled M3h8 body and declared adapter; no new genuine body or adapter was needed. It
runs 22 scenarios over the seven rooms `scrollhall`, `scrollblock`, `dense8`, `gallery`,
`liftroom`, `census` and `doorstep`, 1,372 tics in all: 20 scenarios at medium skill,
which the Rust replay reads, and 2 C-only census rows at easy and hard skill
(`spawn_filter_easy`, `spawn_filter_hard`). The strict build and both sanitizer builds
(the flags are recorded in the contract's `sanitizer_comparison.rule`) produced
byte-identical raw traces, whose SHA-256 the contract records as
`sanitizer_comparison.raw_trace_sha256`:
`8de1ba7d4141e47c000d6ff2a111d8d1887118f789bf53f93f3b8e6341a7f12e`. No sanitizer report
was produced. The two scenarios `fireball_through_blocking_scroller` and
`pillar_straddles_door_step` and the `doorstep` room were added by the independent
review (see that section); every earlier case keeps its index, map and rows, and the
first implementing session's 20-scenario traces had the raw digest
`b18cdaacbddd848735d15eb3bd3782f2b10eb8990c8205c7f7b4561dbec877bc`.

**Evidence change for the alcove.** `monster_hitscan_through_pillar` first placed its
Zombieman (radius 20) at (−536, −64) in a 48 × 40 alcove whose walls its closed box
touched. The Rust replay found that the diagonal policies' spawn rule refuses that
placement (`MonsterError::Arithmetic`), while `P_SpawnMapThing` checks no position. The
spawn rule was kept; the alcove was widened to 48 × 48, four units clear of the box, and
the C evidence was regenerated with the scenario's assertions unchanged (one shot whose
aim and shoot traces skip the pillar, 0 player hits). The 20-scenario digest above is
that of the regenerated traces. The reasoning is in the investigation's
[§12](../M3H9_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md#12-follow-ups-risks-status).

## Independent DSIM36 projection

`tools/reference/m3h9_snapshot_goldens.py --check` projects DSIM36 from the C rows alone
(`e1m1_snapshot.py`, extending the frozen DSIM35 projection without editing it): 337
captures of the 20 medium scenarios (290 before the review scenarios) in
`tests/reference/m3h9_snapshots.csv`, the ordered
events in `m3h9_events.dref`, 15 full-byte `m3h9_state_*.bin` fixtures and
`m3h9_snapshot_contract.json`. The two C-only census rows are not projected. The
generator asserts that every capture equals the DSIM35 projection of the same rows
except the version, the profile value and the context bytes, and that the event streams
are identical. These bytes were committed before the Rust writer and are never derived
from Rust output.

## Rust source replay

`e1m1_replay` runs the M3h8 comparison bodies over the M3h9 rows and goldens, through
`tools/reference/check_player_e1m1.py`. All 20 medium scenarios match through their last
op. The replay's summary line, after the review scenarios and the relaxation of finding 6
below, was:

```text
cases=20 ops=1384 event_frames=1384 event_records=23331 captures=337 fixtures=15 thinker_rows=8982 crossings=12 uses=24 blocked=10 digest=ed25a30faff9a977
```

Before the review the 18 scenarios gave
`cases=18 ops=1167 event_frames=1167 event_records=20615 captures=290 fixtures=15 thinker_rows=8371 crossings=11 uses=18 blocked=10 digest=bdb84ff1bf22f128`.

That is every C row the replay reads, every PBE1 event frame and, at every capture,
the independent digest, size and field count, the 15 fixtures byte for byte and the
`Diagnostic::decode_e1m1` round trip with `decode_movers` rejecting the bytes. The Rust
DSIM36 writer needed no change to produce these bytes. Before the alcove change, the
replay of the 17 scenarios that then loaded matched all 1,006 of their event frames and
all 268 of their captures. A Rust-only test rebuilds the touching alcove in memory and
asserts the refusal with the state unchanged.

## Rust-only policy tests

These are Rust-only safety and policy tests, not source evidence. They are listed in
[TESTING](../TESTING.md#original-two-sided-scrollers-static-decorations-and-the-spawn-filter-c-evidence)
and described in the investigation's §12 status:

- every committed M3h8 scenario replayed under both the movers and the E1M1 policy, with
  the captures and events equal field for field apart from the policy identity;
  DSIM36 rejected by every older reader and `decode_e1m1` reading 36 only;
- the committed `scrollhall`, `scrollblock` and `dense8` rooms loading under the E1M1
  policy only, `dense9` refused by the window rule, and direct simulations of the use
  stop, the player crossing, the blocking line, the eight-line window and the monster's
  `spechit` reset;
- the decoration list, the spawn draws, the census filter, blocking, pass-through,
  fireball, hitscan, ride and door tests on the gallery and liftroom rooms;
- rejections of barrels, hanging corpses and every other unadmitted `info.c` type with a
  DoomEd number, of each decoration type under wires 18 and 19, of a 33rd decoration,
  65 scrollers, every crusher line and the fixed medium single-player options; a
  filtered decoration
  drawing nothing and a filtered barrel not type-checked;
- spawn-batch rollback after accepted monsters, pickups and decorations
  (`DecorationLimit`, `UnsupportedType`, `WorkLimit`);
- refusals of a push, a target, a removal and a state entry on every decoration; under
  wire 20 a target and a removal were accepted until `4c2e056`, after which every tic was
  refused or the capture failed `decode_e1m1`; this was found while writing the fuzz
  target;
- `decode_e1m1` accepting the 15 pinned fixtures alone and rejecting a capture mutated in
  each decoration check, with a lump swap or changed THING options, or with a 33rd
  decoration (`DiagnosticError::Limit`);
- 2,400-tic soaks with four seeds on the five gallery and two liftroom rooms, and the
  §5.3 budget formulas as asserted constants.

Test totals observed by the implementing session after plan step 10:
`cargo test --workspace --locked` 1,010 passed, 0 failed, 3 ignored; the release run
977 passed, 0 failed, 1 ignored. These are not the final gate run.
