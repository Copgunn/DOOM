# M3h9 evidence record

Observed results of the M3h9 milestone (`PlayerMonstersE1M1V1`, wire 20, DSIM36), the
first part (M3h9a) of the split recommended in the
[investigation](../M3H9_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md) §8. Only observations appear here.
The investigation owns the source facts, the admitted domain, the budgets (§5.3), the
decisions (§6) and the plan (§9);
[format](../STATE_FORMAT.md#dsim-v36--original-two-sided-scrollers-and-static-decorations)
owns DSIM36 and
[support](../COMPATIBILITY.md#original-monsters-with-two-sided-scrolling-walls-and-static-decorations)
the admitted behavior. Exact review state belongs to GitHub. No full-player,
whole-ticker, DOS-demo or whole-game equivalence is claimed, and E1M1 itself does not
load under this policy.

**This record is incomplete.** It holds what the implementing session observed through
plan step 10 (the branch up to `bc06aed`): the C evidence, the independent projection, the Rust
replay, the Rust-only tests and the fuzz target with its seed replay; and the
independent review's findings and their fixes (plan step 12), with the regenerated
evidence. The fuzz campaigns, matched timings, local gates and CI have not been run;
their sections below say so, and nothing in them may be read as a pass. The replay, test and fuzzing
sections are correctness observations, not performance measurements, and no time in them
is a measurement.

## IWAD counts

The per-skill THING census of the owner's two IWADs was read locally and read-only on
2026-11-03 with `tools/iwad_counts.py --things-markdown`, and again on 2026-11-04 after
the review added the angle-octant counts (the six other modes and every earlier line of
the two THING modes were byte-identical before and after); the counts-only output is in
[M3H9_IWAD_COUNTS.md](M3H9_IWAD_COUNTS.md). On skill 3 single player E1M1 spawns 91
things: 6 monsters, 52 pickups, 12 solid and 15 non-solid decorations and 6 barrels; 45
of the 91 have an angle octant of 4..7 (44 on easy, 52 on hard), outside the inherited
`[-4,3]` spawn-angle rule.
No IWAD content enters the repository, CI or logs.

## Original-C evidence

`tools/reference/m3h9_reference.py --check --overflow-check --address-check` (with
`ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`, so LeakSanitizer was disabled, not passed) reuses every
compiled M3h8 body and declared adapter; no new genuine body or adapter was needed. It
runs 22 scenarios over the seven rooms `scrollhall`, `scrollblock`, `dense8`, `gallery`,
`liftroom`, `census` and `doorstep`, 1,372 tics in all: 20 scenarios at medium skill,
which the Rust replay reads, and 2 C-only census rows at easy and hard skill
(`spawn_filter_easy`, `spawn_filter_hard`). The strict build and both sanitizer builds
(the flags are recorded in the contract's `sanitizer_comparison.rule`) produced
byte-identical raw traces, whose SHA-256 the contract records as
`sanitizer_comparison.raw_trace_sha256`:
`8de1ba7d4141e47c000d6ff2a111d8d1887118f789bf53f93f3b8e6341a7f12e`. No sanitizer report
was produced. The two scenarios `fireball_through_blocking_scroller` and
`pillar_straddles_door_step` and the `doorstep` room were added by the independent
review (see that section); every earlier case keeps its index, map and rows, and the
first implementing session's 20-scenario traces had the raw digest
`b18cdaacbddd848735d15eb3bd3782f2b10eb8990c8205c7f7b4561dbec877bc`.

**Evidence change for the alcove.** `monster_hitscan_through_pillar` first placed its
Zombieman (radius 20) at (−536, −64) in a 48 × 40 alcove whose walls its closed box
touched. The Rust replay found that the diagonal policies' spawn rule refuses that
placement (`MonsterError::Arithmetic`), while `P_SpawnMapThing` checks no position. The
spawn rule was kept; the alcove was widened to 48 × 48, four units clear of the box, and
the C evidence was regenerated with the scenario's assertions unchanged (one shot whose
aim and shoot traces skip the pillar, 0 player hits). The 20-scenario digest above is
that of the regenerated traces. The reasoning is in the investigation's
[§12](../M3H9_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md#12-follow-ups-risks-status).

The example medians reproduce at about +3%. `death_transition` does not (+3.7% median,
+1.9% minimum). The two armored idle workloads do, and read higher: `player_armor
green_idle` +15.5% median and +8.4% minimum, `blue_idle` +10.8% and +10.5% (+9.0%/+6.9% and

tic for one idle armored player.
Callgrind, with collection limited to `Simulation::advance` and its callees:

- **Instructions** grow 0.7–1.1%: `player_armor green_idle` 1,133,695 → 1,141,931
  (+0.7%), `player_world_pickups medikit` 911,226 → 919,313 (+0.9%), `death_transition`
  1,784,241 → 1,803,282 (+1.1%), `player_heights ceiling_fireball` 3,792,775 → 3,819,814
  (+0.7%), `player_pistol moving_fire` 2,557,396 → 2,584,388 (+1.1%), about 80–270
  instructions a tic. They come from two predicates the compiler no longer inlines once the
  wire-20 variant joins their `match` arms: `Simulation::is_roster_monster` (the roster
  test, 23 instructions a tic) and `Simulation::gibbed` (`has_movers()` now covers wire 20;
  20–40 a tic), plus a few instructions in each preflight from the wider policy matches
  (`preflight_expanded_demons_for`, `preflight_world_pickups`, `state_role_for`).
  Allocator calls inside `advance` are identical in both trees.
- **The rest is layout, not work.** With callgrind's cache and branch simulation,
  `medikit` shows no growth in instruction-cache misses (51,118 → 50,879) but indirect
  mispredictions rise 389 → 851 and conditional ones 5,136 → 5,826; `green_idle` reads
  416 → 619 and 6,710 → 6,869. The new mispredictions fall on functions whose
  instruction and call counts are unchanged (for example the pistol state machine's
  `enter`, 17,693 instructions in both, conditional mispredictions 108 → 180), which is
  what moved code addresses do to a predictor. These examples time 100–300 µs, so a few
  hundred mispredictions are several percent.

The +3% example medians are therefore about 1% added instructions, with the remainder
attributed to code placement. That attribution rests on callgrind's simulated predictor,
not on hardware counters (`perf` is not installed on this host), and it is weakest for the
armored idle workloads, whose +8–15% is far above their +0.7% instructions. No source change
was made for them; forcing the two predicates inline is a possible follow-up.
