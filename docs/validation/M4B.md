# M4b evidence record

Observed results of the M4b milestone (Episode 1 completion: teleporters, the Baron of Hell,
`A_BossDeath`, hard skill, `DEMO1`). Only observations appear here. The
[M4b investigation](../M4B_REFERENCE_INVESTIGATION.md) owns the source facts, the decisions
(§5, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-10) and the plan; [testing](../TESTING.md#m4b-synthetic-mechanism-wads)
owns the commands. Exact review state and CI belong to GitHub.

**Scope of the record.** Phase 2 step 1 (2026-11-10, America/Los_Angeles): synthetic mechanism
WADs run through the genuine engine. Phase 2 step 2 (2026-11-10): the independent DSIM40
projection of those runs and its goldens. Phase 2 step 3 (2026-11-10): wire 24 plumbing; the
Rust results are in [Wire 24 against the genuine engine](#wire-24-against-the-genuine-engine). Phase 2
step 4 (2026-11-10): teleportation, in [Rust replay: teleportation](#rust-replay-teleportation). Phase 2 step 5
(2026-11-10): the Baron and `A_BossDeath`, in [Rust replay: the Baron and A_BossDeath](#rust-replay-the-baron-and-a_bossdeath),
and the second synthetic set (two teleports in one tic). Phase 2 step 6 (2026-11-10): hard skill, in
[Rust replay: hard skill](#rust-replay-hard-skill). Phase 2 step 7 (2026-11-13): the real-map runs, `DEMO1`
and the saw-bite fix, in [Real maps and DEMO1](#real-maps-and-demo1), with SAWBITE added to the second
synthetic set. Address-checked builds ran with `ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`,
so LeakSanitizer was disabled, not passed. Timings quoted here were taken on a host that was
not idle and are not performance measurements.

## Synthetic mechanism WADs (CI evidence)

Everything in this section is synthetic geometry generated by `tools/reference/m4b_wad.py`; no
IWAD was read. Fourteen WADs and 18 scripts (`tests/reference/m4b_manifest.json`). Every
script was run in the three contract variants of the M4b runner (`strict`: `-O0 -fwrapv`;
`overflow`: `-fno-wrapv` with `-fsanitize=signed-integer-overflow,integer-divide-by-zero,float-cast-overflow`;
`address`: `-fsanitize=address,undefined`; gcc 15.2.0, x86-64) and, locally, in the opt-in
`strict32` build (`-m32`, genuine text, no adapter). Commands:

```sh
python3 tools/reference/m4b_build.py --check
python3 tools/reference/m4b_wad.py --check            # also runs m4b_check_wad.py on each WAD
python3 tools/reference/m4b_check_wad.py --check
python3 tools/reference/m4b_scripts.py --check
python3 tools/reference/m4b_reference.py --work /mnt/d/scratch/m4b/ref --check --selftest 30
python3 tools/reference/m4b_reference.py --work /mnt/d/scratch/m4b/ref --strict32   # local only
```

**Variants and the telefrag adapter.** For all 18 scripts the strict, overflow and address dump
sets were byte-identical (SHA-256 of the concatenated snapshots), and so were the strict32 dump
sets when run locally (`-m32` builds and runs on this host). Five scripts contain telefrags
(TFRAG1 three, BOSS1, BOSS2 and BOSSM1 two each): without the declared adapter at
`p_inter.c:817` the overflow and address builds would abort there (section 2 T8.9 of the
investigation); with it they reproduce the strict and strict32 values exactly, so the adapter is
exercised and changes no dump byte. The self-test (`--selftest 30` on TELE1) reported the
planted extra draw at `tic 30: row kind H #0 column prndindex` and the changed command at tic
30: pass. Wall time of the full `--check` run: about 45 s of builds and 140 s of runs on this
host (not idle).

## Independent projection

Phase 2 step 2 (2026-11-10). The strict dumps of the 18 scripts above are projected to DSIM40,
the schema of wire 24 ([STATE_FORMAT](../STATE_FORMAT.md#dsim-v40--episode-1-part-2-teleporters-the-baron-of-hell-a_bossdeath-and-hard-skill)),
by `tools/reference/level_snapshot_m4b.py` (profile `episode1v2`). That module imports the frozen
DSIM39 projection of `level_snapshot.py` and does not edit it. It changes the version word,
`profile` and `context.schema`, inserts `level.map`, admits skill 3, and adds refusals for
anything in a dump it does not understand: a row kind or column outside the M4a driver's
contract, a game state other than a single-player level, and an actor state outside its type's
normal closure. No Rust output is read. Commands are in
[TESTING](../TESTING.md#m4b-synthetic-mechanism-wads):

```sh
python3 tools/reference/m4b_reference.py --work /mnt/d/scratch/m4b/ref --check --keep-dumps
python3 tools/reference/m4b_snapshot_goldens.py --dumps /mnt/d/scratch/m4b/ref/runs --check --selftest
python3 tools/reference/test_level_snapshot_m4b.py
```

## Wire 24 against the genuine engine

Phase 2 step 3 (2026-11-10). Wire 24 (`PlayerMonstersEpisode1V2`, DSIM40) so far is wire 23
under a new identity, so the evidence asks one question: does wire 24 run every M4a synthetic
script exactly as the genuine engine does, in DSIM40? No M4b script can run yet: every one
needs special 97, doomednum 14, doomednum 3003 or skill 3. Each is refused at load, as

**C expectations.** `m4a_reference.py --check --keep-dumps` ran on this host. All 30 scripts
gave dump sets identical across the strict, overflow and address variants, and
`m4a_synthetic_results.json` matched. `m4b_m4a_goldens.py --compare39` then checked each strict
dump set against the frozen `dumps_sha256`. It projected all 15,116 snapshots of the 30 scripts
to DSIM40 with no projection stop, into `tests/reference/m4b_m4a_goldens.json`. On every
snapshot, DSIM40 equalled DSIM39 outside `profile`, `context.schema`, `actors.options` and
`level.map`. It took 6.5 minutes, with the DSIM39 comparison included. No Rust output was read.

**Rust.** `check_player_episode1v2.py --binary target/release/examples/player_level_run --require`

### The M5WALK3 divergence and its fix

The walk-to-exit script on E1M5, skill 3 (`tests/reference/e1m5_scripts/walk_to_exit_skill3.txt`,
9,456 tics) matched the genuine engine through capture 9,152. Rust then refused tic 9,153 with
`PlayerBody(Invariant)`, while the genuine engine continued to the exit at tic 9,455 (variants
identical). It was an explicit refusal, not a silent difference.

**Cause (found by instrumentation).** The player holds the chainsaw; `A_Saw` sets `MF_JUSTATTACKED`
(0x80) on the player's body, only on a hit (p_pspr.c:542). `P_PlayerThink` runs before
`P_RunThinkers` (p_tick.c:150-152), so a Demon or Spectre that bites in the same tic finds the flag
set; the flag is cleared at the next tic (p_user.c:249-255). The wire-23 bite path, `strike_player`
in `crates/doom-sim/src/demon.rs`, admitted only the pristine player flags plus `MF_JUSTHIT` and
`MF_CORPSE`, so the body failed its flag check. Nothing on the C bite path reads
`MF_JUSTATTACKED`: `A_SargAttack` (p_enemy.c:935-948), `A_FaceTarget` (782-796, reads only
`MF_SHADOW`), `P_CheckMeleeRange` (174-192) and `P_DamageMobj` (p_inter.c:775-918). A suspect that
instrumentation ruled out was the wire-24 item-total cross-check.

**Fix (2dd986c).** `has_bite_on_lunging_body()` admits that one extra flag under wire 24 only. It is a
source-equivalence change, with the C citations above.

**Known wire-23 gap, unpatched by rule.** Wire 23 admits the chainsaw and Demons, so the same tic can
arise under it, and wire 23 still refuses it with `PlayerBody(Invariant)` (asserted by
`saw_bite_tests.rs`, which replays SAWBITE under wire 23 and sees tic 74 refused with state unchanged).
It is not patched there, because historical profiles keep their behaviour. It is an explicit
refusal, never a silent difference. None of the wire-23 real-map or synthetic runs reached it. Wires
below 23 do not admit the chainsaw. [COMPATIBILITY](../COMPATIBILITY.md#explicitly-not-admitted) lists it
under wire 23.

**Synthetic reproduction SAWBITE (663d92b, second set).** `tests/reference/m4b_manifest2.json` holds
the scene: a 116-tic script plus marker (117 snapshots), `A_Saw` hits from tic 45, and two Demon bites
on the flagged body at dump tics 73 and 97 (`bite flags&128` is a must-show at tic 73). The three
genuine variants were byte-identical (dump digest
`cef27e453e2eb1df2026a6ea0206d9b2f32331ef4f4cde9926b4e301d946c1e3` in `m4b_synthetic_results2.json`,
`ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0`). The independent DSIM40 projection covers all 117 snapshots, sequence
digest `06769c5acd5530cd`, final `7ec7fd385562fa09`; the fixture is `m4b2_episode1v2_SAWBITE_74.bin`.
The harness was shown able to fail here: the 484fade binary (before the fix) refuses DSIM tic 74
with `PlayerBody(Invariant)`, and with `demon.rs` reverted the mechanism goldens test fails on
